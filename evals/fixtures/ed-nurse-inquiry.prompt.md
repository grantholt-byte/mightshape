# Acceptance fixture: emergency department nurse Inquiry Lab

## User prompt

> We're designing software for emergency department nurses. I don't understand
> their workflow. Help me understand it.

## Starting conditions

- No site, hospital, jurisdiction, or specific emergency department has been
  identified.
- No local nurse has yet been interviewed or observed.
- Current web research is available for the exercise.
- The domain is regulated, consequential, and locally variable; use `DEEP`
  grounding for a synthetic practitioner.

## Exercise boundary

Research and create a source-traceable Reality Packet, then demonstrate a short
story-first Synthetic Practitioner interview under Solution Blackout. Prepare a
human interview/observation strategy and Reality Check. Do not invent a live
public link, local workflow, interview completion, or human quote.
