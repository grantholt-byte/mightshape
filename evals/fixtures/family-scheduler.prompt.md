# Acceptance fixture: family scheduler

## User prompt

> I have an excellent idea for an AI app that automatically plans family
> schedules. Let's build it.

## Starting conditions

- No human interviews, observations, product analytics, or authoritative
  research have been supplied.
- The request is solution-first and behavior-dependent.
- Assume a substantial production build would be expensive, but a manual
  experiment is reversible.
- Use `STANDARD` depth and an appropriately sized panel; do not summon all ten
  members merely to create spectacle.

## Exercise boundary

Run a representative journey from Intake through a Prototype Card and advisory
Build Gate. It is acceptable to stop before collecting unavailable human
evidence. Do not fabricate completion of interviews or experiments. Show what
would be written to project memory.
