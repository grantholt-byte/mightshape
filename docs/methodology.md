# Methodology and attribution

Design Council uses iterative Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test modes inspired
primarily by publicly available Stanford d.school materials. It adds its own Intake
orchestration, Council system, evidence controls, Inquiry Lab, and Build Gate.

It is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by
Stanford University or the Stanford d.school.

The machine-readable method registry labels every method as one of:

- `stanford_dschool`
- `supplemental_design_practice`
- `design_council_original`

Primary citations and lineage notes live in
`skills/design-council/references/source-notes.md`. The repository links to source materials
and uses original summaries. It does not bundle Stanford logos, artwork, PDFs, or substantial
copied passages. A licensing review is required before adapting source cards or protected
materials beyond the current paraphrased references.
