# Methodology and attribution

MightShape independently implements iterative Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test
modes within a human-centered design thinking practice. It adds its own Intake orchestration,
Council system, evidence controls, Inquiry Lab, and Build Gate.

It is an independent product and does not claim affiliation with, endorsement by, certification
from, or authorship by any third-party institution.

The machine-readable method registry labels every method as one of:

- `public_design_practice`
- `supplemental_design_practice`
- `mightshape_original`

The runtime lineage policy lives in `skills/mightshape/references/source-notes.md`; the
maintainer-facing source and rights review lives in `docs/THIRD_PARTY_SOURCES.md` and is excluded
from generated plugin packages. The runtime uses independently written summaries and does not
bundle third-party logos, artwork, course PDFs, worksheets, facilitator scripts, source-card
layouts, or substantial copied passages. Rights review is required before adapting protected
source expression.
