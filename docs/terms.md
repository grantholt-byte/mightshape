# Terms publication placeholder

This repository does not establish public service terms. Current OpenAI guidance makes a terms URL
optional for the skills-only Core. A publisher must host reviewed terms under the verified
publisher's identity before offering the hosted interview service or submitting an MCP-backed
package, and may elect to provide them earlier as a trust asset. Those terms should distinguish
local open-source plugin use from the optional hosted interview service,
allocate responsibility for lawful research/consent, describe prohibited sensitive uses,
state warranty/liability terms consistent with the selected license, and reserve future
Exchange terms for a separate launch.

Do not use this note, or a raw GitHub rendering of it, as a production terms-of-service page
without owner and legal review. The final public page needs the actual publisher identity,
contact route, effective date, governing terms, and deployment-specific service description.
