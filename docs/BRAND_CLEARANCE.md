# MightShape brand-clearance gate

This document is for maintainers preparing a relaunch. It is not legal advice and must not be
used as a claim that the name is available, registrable, or non-infringing.

**MightShape** was selected only after a preliminary exact-name screen. That screen is a useful
product-development filter, not legal clearance. Search results can miss pending applications,
phonetic or conceptual similarities, unregistered/common-law use, relevant translations,
company and domain use, and conflicts within related goods or services.

## Preliminary screen recorded 2026-08-14

- the current USPTO search service returned zero live exact or fuzzy word-mark hits for
  `MIGHTSHAPE` in the queries run;
- an exact quoted commercial-web search found no same-name software or design-research product;
- GitHub repository-name search returned zero repositories;
- Verisign `.com` RDAP returned no current registration record for `mightshape.com`.

These are transient knockout-search signals, not a reservation, registrability opinion, domain
purchase, or comprehensive similarity search. The UK IPO interface required manual CAPTCHA review,
and WIPO warns that database absence does not establish availability. UK, EU, WIPO,
state/company-registry, app-store, social-handle, foreign-language, and common-law review remains
unfinished.

MightShape was preferred to the otherwise expressive **Hunchloom** candidate because Loom is an
established adjacent collaboration-software brand. Avoiding that suffix reduces one obvious risk;
it does not make MightShape legally safe.

Before public relaunch, qualified trademark counsel should review at minimum:

- United States federal and state records plus relevant common-law use;
- United Kingdom records and marketplace use;
- European Union records and relevant member-state considerations;
- confusingly similar spellings, sounds, meanings, and visual presentations—not only exact hits;
- plugin, software, design/research, collaboration, education, consulting, and hosted-service
  goods or services that could overlap the planned offering;
- app stores, plugin directories, company registries, domains, social handles, and major web use;
- the proposed word mark, logo, icon, tagline placement, and any planned Exchange sub-brand.

Counsel should document the searched jurisdictions/classes, material conflicts, residual risk,
filing recommendation, and any geographic or service limitations. The owner has directed the
MightShape release after reviewing the preliminary screen; that decision accepts residual naming
risk and is not a legal-clearance opinion. Do not represent the name as legally safe or federally
registered unless later evidence supports that statement.
