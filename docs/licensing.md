# Licensing decision

The repository currently proposes the MIT License for V1 plugin source. MIT is simple,
widely recognized by both plugin ecosystems, permits commercial and private use, and does
not prevent the owner from operating proprietary hosted services such as a future Exchange.

The tradeoff is material: MIT also permits redistribution and derivative plugins, subject
to preservation of the notice. It does not grant trademark rights, protect the Design
Council name, require improvements to be shared, or prevent competitors from using the code.

Before public beta or marketplace submission, the product owner must deliberately confirm
one of these directions:

1. retain MIT for broad adoption;
2. choose Apache-2.0 for an explicit patent grant;
3. adopt a source-available/custom license after qualified legal review;
4. keep distribution private until the business decision is made.

Do not publish merely because `LICENSE` exists. The publication checklist treats owner
license confirmation as a manual release decision. Changing an already-published license
does not revoke rights to versions distributed under MIT.
