# Licensing decision

The repository uses the MIT License for the private beta plugin source. MIT is simple,
widely recognized by both plugin ecosystems, permits commercial and private use, and does
not prevent the owner from operating proprietary hosted services such as a future Exchange.

The tradeoff is material: MIT also permits redistribution and derivative plugins, subject
to preservation of the notice. It does not grant trademark rights, protect the Design
Council name, require improvements to be shared, or prevent competitors from using the code.

This beta retains MIT as the best fit for low-friction plugin adoption while keeping future
hosted and enterprise services separable. Before public marketplace submission, the owner
may still revisit:

1. whether an explicit trademark policy is needed for the Design Council name;
2. whether a future release should use Apache-2.0 for an explicit patent grant;
3. whether hosted Exchange terms require separate qualified legal review.

The private beta recipient receives this version under MIT. Changing a future release's
license would not revoke rights already granted for versions distributed under MIT.
