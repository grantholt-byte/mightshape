# Code of conduct

Design Council welcomes rigorous disagreement without personal hostility.

Contributors must:

- treat people with dignity across identity, experience, expertise, and communication style;
- critique claims and implementations rather than belittling contributors;
- avoid harassment, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and discriminatory conduct;
- protect participant, project, and security-sensitive information;
- acknowledge uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and mistakes;
- make space for minority positions without manufacturing conflict.

This applies in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces, and events where a
person represents the project. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or maintainers. They should
respond promptly, protect reporters from retaliation, and disclose conflicts when deciding
an outcome.
